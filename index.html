About Me

A Business Mindset. A Digital Focus.

My career is a logical progression of structured thinking and practical business management. It began with a Master’s degree in Economics from the Armenian State University of Economics, where I graduated with honors. This foundation developed my analytical and commercial thinking, teaching me how to evaluate systems, resources, and outcomes with precision.

For over a decade, I worked in Armenia’s public sector, progressing from specialist to chief specialist. This environment demanded rigorous attention to detail and process management. I worked extensively with databases, property management, contracts, privatization, free-use projects, documentation, and operational processes. This experience ingrained in me a deep respect for structured workflows and compliance.

In 2022, I applied this operational discipline to the private sector by starting and operating an independent marketplace business. Over the first two years, the business experienced positive sales growth. This hands-on experience shifted my focus toward the digital realm, revealing the direct impact of user experience and operational efficiency on commercial success.

Today, I am actively developing practical skills in Shopify, e-commerce store architecture, theme customization, Liquid, CSS, product page design, and storefront optimization. My interests lie at the intersection of digital business, operational efficiency, user experience, AI-assisted workflows, and international e-commerce. I do not view this as a career restart, but rather as the natural evolution of my analytical and operational expertise into the digital commerce space.
