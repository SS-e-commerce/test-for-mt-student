About Me

Shopify Specialist based in Spain

I’m Syuzanna, a Shopify specialist based in Spain. I work on stores from both sides: the technical part (Liquid code, custom sections) and the design part (UX/UI, landing pages, how a page feels to a customer).

I have hands-on e-commerce and marketplace experience. Since 2022 I have worked in online sales, product presentation, catalog management, and daily marketplace operations. During that time, sales grew about 41% in the first year and 70% in the second. It taught me what makes people buy: clear product pages, accurate details, and a simple path to checkout.

Today I write custom Liquid, build Shopify sections that fit a brand, and design landing pages for products and campaigns. I also build standalone web pages hosted on GitHub, and I do UX/UI design, so I think about layout and usability, not only code. You can see examples of my work on my GitHub.

Before this, I worked for 12 years at Armenia’s State Property Management Department, where I went from Specialist to Chief Specialist and took part in more than 50 projects. I also have a Master’s degree in Economics. That background made me careful with details, data, and deadlines, and I bring the same habits to client work.
